## English

**Short**

> I brought a slow, cheap TV box back to life. I made a repo with one prompt that does the whole job through an AI assistant (you only answer questions) and a tiny launcher for boxes with little memory. Free and open source: https://github.com/franco-valenzuela/fox-launcher-tvbox

**Long**

> Do you have a generic TV box that freezes all the time? Mine had three factory mistakes: it claimed 4 GB and had 1 GB, its emergency memory never started, and at every power-on it recompiled an app for 10 minutes without ever finishing.
>
> I put together a repo with:
> - A prompt you give to an AI assistant that can use your computer. It checks the box, explains what it found, and asks before each change.
> - Fox Launcher: a 37 KB home screen with no advertising and no Internet access.
> - A guide in 10 languages, for programmers and non-programmers.
>
> Nothing is uninstalled and everything can be undone. It was tested on one box only (RK3229, Android 7.1), so if you try it on another one, please tell me how it went.
>
> https://github.com/franco-valenzuela/fox-launcher-tvbox
>
> Built on the idea of cleanyourtv: https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv

## Before you post
