# Code of conduct

This project is for everyone: people who program and people who do not, of any age.

## Please

- Be kind. Many people here are fixing a box for their family.
- Explain things in simple words. No question is too basic.
- Say what box you tested on and what really happened.
- Give credit when you use someone's work.

## Please do not

- Insult, mock or harass anyone.
- Post personal data, yours or anyone else's: addresses, Wi-Fi names, accounts.
- Post or ask for apps that give access to films, series or TV channels without permission from their owners.
- Advertise things that have nothing to do with the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report the comment with GitHub's own "Report content" button, or write to the maintainer through a private security report (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Comments that break these rules can be hidden or removed, and repeat offenders can be blocked.
