Have a different box? Lists for hundreds of Android TV models live in [cleanyourtv](https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv/tree/main/examples). If your box is not there either, share your list in [Discussions](https://github.com/franco-valenzuela/fox-launcher-tvbox/discussions) once you have lived with it for a few days.
